ELIRA is an AI backend built for legal-tech workflows. It processes videos, audio, PDFs, and
images through a single pipeline: Whisper for transcription, BART for summarisation, spaCy for
named-entity recognition, sentiment analysis, and OCR for scanned/image-based documents — all
packaged as a scalable, Docker-ready microservice.
